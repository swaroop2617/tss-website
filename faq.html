FAQ

How can I join the Technical Student Society?

Students can join by filling the registration form available on the "Contact" page and attending the induction session.

Are there any fees for membership?

No, membership for students is completely free.

Can non-engineering students participate?

Yes, our society welcomes students from all streams interested in technology and innovation.

How do I know about upcoming events?

Upcoming events and announcements are regularly updated on the "Events" page and through our newsletter.
